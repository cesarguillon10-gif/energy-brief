Learn one thing

A key idea for the role

Italy's MACSE storage auctions

  • MACSE (Italy's grid-scale storage auction) gives long-term contracts for big batteries.
  • Winners get fixed payments, so revenue risk moves from developers to the system.
  • Awards are concentrated in a few players, such as BW ESS and A2A.

→It shows how Italy is buying flexibility at grid scale, which sets the price Axle's home devices compete with.
