Learn one thing

A key idea for the role

Netherlands congestion management with GOPACS

  • GOPACS is a platform where grid operators buy flexibility to ease local grid congestion.
  • TenneT (the Dutch transmission operator) and the regional operators use it together.
  • Flexible assets are paid to shift demand or output away from congested times.

→The Netherlands has severe grid congestion, so it is a likely early market for Axle.
