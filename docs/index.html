Learn one thing

A key idea for the role

Revenue per device: EV charger vs heat pump

  • An EV charger moves a large, flexible load, often 7 kW, for hours each night.
  • A heat pump load depends on weather and must protect comfort, so it shifts less.
  • Revenue per device comes from shifted kWh times price spread, plus any service payments.

→You will model revenue per device for each new country, so know which device earns more and why.
