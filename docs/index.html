Learn one thing

A key idea for the role

Germany's balancing markets: FCR, aFRR, mFRR

  • FCR (frequency containment reserve) reacts in seconds and is paid for capacity.
  • aFRR (automatic frequency restoration reserve) follows a grid signal, within minutes.
  • mFRR (manual frequency restoration reserve) is called by the operator, slower, and easier for small fleets.

→Knowing which product a home battery fleet can serve helps you model German revenue per device.
